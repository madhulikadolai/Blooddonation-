📞 Quick Contact

⚡ How BloodBridge Works

1️⃣ Register

A donor registers with their blood group and availability.

2️⃣ Request

A person in need submits a blood request.

3️⃣ Match

BloodBridge searches for available matching donors.

4️⃣ Connect

The requester can contact a matching donor.
